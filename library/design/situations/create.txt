Situation: Create

Use Create when the work does not exist yet and no product around it supplies an established design. You are building from a brief, so the brief is your starting material: its goal, required behavior, content, and explicit constraints. Anything the brief supplies—content, identity, assets, a typeface or a palette—is a constraint and is kept in the step that owns it; every other decision is open, and freedom applies only to decisions that are open. Work from the inside out. First state what people arrive needing to understand or do, then establish the content, objects, and actions that answer it, then decide which regions should exist and in what order. Only then name the job each section must do, look at how real products do each job, and adapt the component they use. Visual decisions come after that structure exists: research the open pillars from human-made sources, then set the type, produce the imagery, and construct the color with the real content in place. Lay out from the rendered content dimensions, connect behavior and responsive adaptation, and verify by walking the real task at desktop, tablet, and phone widths. Nothing is chosen from memory or habit; a component picked before looking, a remembered palette, or a familiar page template is a gap in the work, not a decision. When verification finds a discrepancy, return to the responsible step and carry the correction forward from there.

May change: Establish the unresolved structure, controls, interactions, composition, typography, palette, imagery, and other decisions needed for the assigned scope.
Must preserve: The brief, supplied content and identity, technical constraints, and required relationships with surrounding work. Freedom applies to decisions that are open.

SOP

1. State the question and the end goal [process 0]
Id: question
From the brief, state what people arrive needing to understand or do, and the observable condition that will hold when the work is complete. List what the brief supplies—content, identity, assets, and constraints. These are kept in the step that owns them.

2. Establish the content and actions [process 1]
Id: content
Needs: question
Establish the objects, actions, states, and decision prerequisites the brief requires. Create the fictional or supplied content, including its long, short, empty, unavailable, and error variations, before any dimensions are settled.

3. Decide what should exist and rank it [process 2]
Id: order
Needs: question, content
Apply the existence question to every proposed region and section, then rank the information and actions for each task.

4. Name the jobs and adapt components [process 3]
Id: components
Needs: content, order
Name the job each section must do, look at how real products do each job, then adapt the component they use. Derive control semantics and state-dependent wording. A custom component needs its stated reason.

5. Research the open pillars [process 4]
Id: research
Needs: question, components
Research every pillar the brief leaves unresolved from human-made sources and assemble the aesthetic configuration. Supplied identity is kept, and new ingredients must work with it.

6. Set the typography [process 5]
Id: typography
Needs: order, components, research
Select and set type unless the brief supplies it; supplied type is kept and still rendered with the real content to give layout its widths.

7. Produce the imagery [process 6]
Id: imagery
Needs: research, typography
Produce one representative asset beside the actual text, resolve it, then produce the pack from it. If imagery contributes nothing to the communication and the brief does not require it, state that and let type and space carry the design.

8. Construct the color [process 7]
Id: color
Needs: order, research, imagery
Construct the palette unless the brief supplies one; a supplied palette is kept and still receives roles and readable pairs.

9. Lay out the views [process 8]
Id: layout
Needs: order, components, typography, imagery, color
Construct the arrangement from the rendered content dimensions, building each section from the components adapted in SOP step 4.

10. Complete the behavior [process 9]
Id: behavior
Needs: content, components, layout
Implement every action, state, responsive change, and purposeful motion.

11. Verify and correct [process 10] (verify)
Id: verify
Needs: question, behavior
Produces: What was checked walking the task at desktop, tablet, and phone widths, with nothing left to correct.
Follow the task through entry, decision, action, feedback, and return at desktop, tablet, and phone widths. Record it as done, or revise the operation that works in the procedure step responsible for the earliest discrepancy.

Completion: Deliver the working output, the command or access needed to use it, final captures saved inside the work folder, and a concise account of what was checked and any remaining limitations.
