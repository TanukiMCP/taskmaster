Scope: Page or view

Use Page or view when the decisions to change lie within one screen and the sections and components on it. A page answers the question people have when they arrive, from the places they actually arrive from, and leads them on to where they go next. The page decides which sections exist and in what order, so the existence question is asked here: every section must answer part of the page's question, and content that belongs with an object travels with it instead of getting its own section. Decide what belongs in the initial view by what people need first, not by what was there before. Build each section from components adapted from real products that do the same job. Shared navigation is retained unless the page cannot work with it, and product-wide foundations are retained identity unless the assignment opens them or the page needs a treatment they cannot serve. Imagery is framed for each width and delivered at the sizes it is shown. Verify the page from where people actually arrive, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths, through its onward connections; neighboring destinations are checked only for working connections, not redesigned.

Parent: Flow or Product
Included: Its composition, content organization, sections and their existence, local controls, imagery, responsive treatment, states, entry, and onward connections.
Boundary: Neighboring destinations, the entire journey, and global restyling are not automatically included.
Record in step 0: Where people arrive from and where the page leads.

At this scope
Question: The question a person has on arriving at this page, from the places they arrive from.
Content and order: The page's content, local actions, states, decision prerequisites, entry, and onward connections; which sections should exist; the order across the page's regions and within each, including what belongs in the initial view.
Components: The page's sections, the job each must do, the real products inspected for each job, the components adapted from them, local controls, collection structures, and state-dependent wording. Shared navigation is retained unless the page cannot work with it.
Foundations: The pillars the page opens. Product-wide foundations are retained identity unless the assignment opens them or the page needs a treatment they cannot serve. The page's text roles include any display setting it needs; its imagery and its framing at each width are set for the page; its color uses, areas, and emphasis are set for the page.
Layout: The page's composition at each width, its grid, spacing, regions, icons, and surfaces.
Behavior: The page's states, feedback, responsive changes, image delivery, and motion, and its entry and onward connections.
Verify: The page reached from where people actually arrive, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths, through its onward connections. Neighboring destinations are checked only for working connections.

Expansion: If the page cannot work without changing a neighboring destination, the journey, or a shared foundation, identify the specific conflict and the smallest required expansion.
