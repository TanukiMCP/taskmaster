Scope: Shared styles and assets

Select when the decisions the task needs to change belong to a foundation applied across the product rather than to one place: typography, palette, illustration family, iconography, surfaces, motion, or the shared visual identity.

Included: The named foundation, its relationships, and its application across intended uses. This may be typography, palette, illustration family, iconography, surfaces, motion, or the shared visual identity.

Boundary: Other foundations and independent page redesigns stay outside scope. Directly affected wrapping, spacing, framing, or presentation can change as necessary to apply the assigned foundation.

Record in step 0 which foundation or foundations are named and the intended uses the foundation must serve.

Step limits

0 Question: What the uses of this foundation must communicate, and the question each kind of use answers for the people using it.
1 Content: Keep. Record the inventory of intended uses: each kind of text, image, icon, surface, color, or movement the foundation serves, with its range of content and states.
2 Order: Keep. Record the order each kind of use must express, so the foundation can serve it.
3 Components: Keep. Record the components, controls, and states the foundation must distinguish.
4 Research: Decide for the named foundation. Retain the other foundations as identity and make the new ingredient work with them.
5 Typography: Decide when typography is named; otherwise keep.
6 Imagery: Decide when the illustration family or imagery is named; otherwise keep.
7 Color: Decide when the palette is named; otherwise keep, and still check readable pairs where the named foundation changes what color sits beside.
8 Layout: Decide icons or surfaces when named. Otherwise change only the directly affected wrapping, spacing, framing, or presentation needed to apply the foundation; page compositions are kept.
9 Behavior: Decide motion when named. Otherwise keep, checking responsive behavior where the foundation changes dimensions.
10 Verify: A representative sample of every kind of intended use, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths, including the densest and most constrained uses.

Expansion: If applying the foundation requires redesigning a page or changing another foundation, identify the specific conflict and the smallest required expansion.
