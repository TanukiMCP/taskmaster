Scope: Product

Select when the decisions the task needs to change span the product's structure, its connected journeys, and its shared foundations together.

Included: Product-wide structure, connected journeys, shared design foundations, and relationships among the parts.

Boundary: Scope does not authorize a new product purpose, invented requirements, removal of required capabilities, or unrelated infrastructure replacement.

Record in step 0 the product's purpose, its audiences, and its main tasks.

Step limits

0 Question: The question each main audience arrives with, and the question each main task answers.
1 Content: The product-wide objects, actions, states, decision prerequisites, and the journeys that connect them.
2 Order: The order for each main task and the navigation that connects them.
3 Components: The sections across the product, the job each must do, the components adapted from real products for those jobs, and consistent components and conventions for equivalent jobs.
4 Research: Every pillar the situation opens, resolved once as shared foundations for the whole product.
5 Typography: The shared type system, then its application to each main view.
6 Imagery: The shared imagery style and pack, then each view's assets.
7 Color: The shared palette and roles, then each view's uses.
8 Layout: The shared grid, spacing, icon, and surface relationships, then each main view constructed from them.
9 Behavior: Every journey's states, feedback, interruption, recovery, persistence, responsive changes, and motion.
10 Verify: Each main journey end to end, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths, including empty, error, and unavailable states, and whether the parts read as one product.

Shared foundations are resolved once in steps 4–7 before the per-view work of steps 5–8 spreads. When the product is large, Taskmaster may run the shared foundations as one tasklist and each main view or journey as a following tasklist that keeps those foundations.

Expansion: If the goal requires a new product purpose, a removed capability, or infrastructure replacement, identify the specific conflict and stop for the user's decision.
