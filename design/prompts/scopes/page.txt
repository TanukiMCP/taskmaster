Scope: Page or view

Select when the decisions the task needs to change lie within one page, screen, or view and the components on it.

Included: Its composition, content organization, local controls, imagery, responsive treatment, states, entry, and onward connections.

Boundary: Neighboring destinations, the entire journey, and global restyling are not automatically included.

Record in step 0 where people arrive from and where the page leads.

Step limits

0 Question: The question a person has on arriving at this page, from the places they arrive from.
1 Content: The page's content, local actions, states, decision prerequisites, entry, and onward connections.
2 Order: The order across the page's regions and within each, including what belongs in the initial view.
3 Components: The page's sections, the components each section needs and their jobs, their established sources, local controls, collection structures, and state-dependent wording. Shared navigation is retained unless the page cannot work with it.
4 Research: The pillars the page opens. Product-wide foundations are retained identity unless the assignment opens them or the page needs a treatment they cannot serve.
5 Typography: The page's text roles, including any display setting it needs.
6 Imagery: The page's imagery and its framing at each width.
7 Color: The page's color uses, areas, and emphasis.
8 Layout: The page's composition at each width, its grid, spacing, regions, icons, and surfaces.
9 Behavior: The page's states, feedback, responsive changes, image delivery, and motion, and its entry and onward connections.
10 Verify: The page reached from where people actually arrive, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths, through its onward connections. Neighboring destinations are checked only for working connections.

Expansion: If the page cannot work without changing a neighboring destination, the journey, or a shared foundation, identify the specific conflict and the smallest required expansion.
