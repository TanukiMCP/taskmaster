Scope: Flow

Select when the decisions the task needs to change span the connected views of one named task: booking, checkout, signup, onboarding, editing, or recovery.

Included: The connected decisions, views, controls, states, feedback, interruption, recovery, and return needed for the named task.

Boundary: Other journeys and nearby shared areas remain outside scope unless needed by the assigned task.

Record in step 0 the task's entry points, its commitment points, and where it returns.

Step limits

0 Question: The question the whole task answers, and the question at each decision point along it.
1 Content: Every decision, view, action, state, and decision prerequisite in the task, including interruption, recovery, return, and what persists between steps.
2 Order: The order within each view and across the sequence, so that what is needed for each commitment is available before it.
3 Components: The job at each decision and view, the components and controls adapted from real products for those jobs, the commitment points, and wording that distinguishes unsaved, pending, and committed states across the sequence.
4 Research: The pillars the flow opens. Product-wide foundations are retained identity unless the assignment opens them.
5 Typography: The text roles across the flow's views, consistent from step to step.
6 Imagery: Imagery within the flow's views.
7 Color: Color uses and state roles across the flow, consistent from step to step.
8 Layout: Each view in the flow, with shared relationships that keep the sequence recognizable.
9 Behavior: Feedback, validation, interruption, recovery, return, persistence, responsive changes, and transitions between steps.
10 Verify: The whole task from each entry point through every commitment, including going back, interruption, error recovery, and return, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths.

Expansion: If the task cannot work without changing another journey or a shared area, identify the specific conflict and the smallest required expansion.
