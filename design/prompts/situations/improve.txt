Situation: Improve

Select when existing work is being reconsidered for quality or suitability and no single specific problem defines the task. Improve reconsiders quality or suitability.

The agent may change: Reconsider weak design decisions within scope, including hierarchy, composition, controls, navigation, typography, palette, imagery, copy, and interaction behavior. Substantial redesign is possible.

It must preserve: Required capabilities, factual meaning, user data, explicit constraints, and useful connections to the surrounding product. A visual-improvement task does not authorize inventing services or dropping requirements.

Whether the existing identity is open is a task condition. Record it in step 0. When identity is not open, steps 4–7 keep it and research only what the identity does not already resolve.

Entry: Step 0, then step 10 on the existing work.

Diagnosis

After step 0, run step 10 on the existing work as it stands. Follow the task through entry, decision, action, feedback, and return; read what attracts attention first, what appears grouped, and what can be read, at desktop, tablet, and phone widths. Record each discrepancy with the question and the step responsible for it, using step 10's cause list. The earliest responsible step is k.

Step rules

0 Question: Decide. State the question, the end goal, and which existing decisions are constraints. Record whether identity is open.
Steps before k: Keep. Name each kept decision and why it still serves the question.
Step k and every later step: Decide. Reconsider the decision with the step's method. The result may retain an existing treatment where it still fits; state that as a kept decision with its reason. A later step is reconsidered because its inputs changed, not only because it was weak.
10 Verify: Decide. Check the improved work against the question and the diagnosis. Return the responsible step or done. A newly found earlier cause becomes the new k.

Completion: Step 10 returns done. Deliver the improved work, how to reach it, before and after captures saved inside the work folder, and a concise account of what changed, what was checked, and any remaining limitations.
